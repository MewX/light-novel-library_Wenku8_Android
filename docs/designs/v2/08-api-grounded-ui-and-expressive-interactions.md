## Part 2: Where Material 3 Expressive Interactions Happen for Light Novel & Comic/Illustration Styles

Japanese Light Novels (文库本) differ fundamentally from plain-text webnovels: every volume combines **literary typography** with **color frontispieces (`口绘`, often 2-page wide spreads)** and **full-page black-and-white character/scene illustrations (`插图`)**. Here is where M3 Expressive interactions bring that experience to life:

### 1. Inline `<!--image-->` Art Cards → Shared-Bounds Comic/Artbook Lightbox (`UnifiedReaderScreen`)
- **In-Chapter Presentation**: When `WenkuReaderLoaderXML` parses `<!--image-->...<!--image-->`, the Reader renders an **M3 Expressive Art Card** (`20dp` rounded corners, subtle tonal frame, caption `插图 03`, and a floating pill `🖼 轻触展开插图画集 (12P)`).
- **Expressive Shared-Bounds Expansion**: Tapping or pinching-out on any inline illustration triggers a **`SharedTransitionLayout` Spatial Spring (`dampingRatio = 0.72f, stiffness = 380f`)** that expands the image seamlessly into the **In-Reader Artbook & Comic Spread Overlay** without launching a separate Activity or losing your reading place.
- **Double-Page Spread Mode (`跨页拼合` for Light Novel `口绘`)**: Light novel color frontispieces (`口绘 01–02`) are frequently split into two consecutive vertical JPEG URLs by Wenku8. A 1-tap **`跨页拼合: 开` (`SegmentedButton` / `FilterChip`)** stitches paired landscape/spread pages side-by-side (supporting right-to-left Japanese order or left-to-right order) with zero seam gap and 4× pinch-to-zoom!
- **M3 Multi-Browse Thumbnail Scrubber Strip**: At the bottom of the Artbook overlay, all illustrations in the current volume are displayed in an **M3 `HorizontalMultiBrowseCarousel`** thumbnail strip where the currently focused illustration dynamically expands width (`104dp`) while adjacent thumbnails compress (`76dp`).

### 2. Volume-Level `<chapter>插图</chapter>` Quick-Jump (`BookshelfScreen` & `NovelDetailScreen`)
- Because `Wenku8API.getNovelIndex` (`do=list`) places a dedicated `<chapter>插图</chapter>` at the end of almost every volume, `v2.0` automatically detects this chapter and surfaces a **`tertiaryContainer` `🖼 插图` AssistChip** right on the collapsed Volume row (Board 03) and on the Bookshelf detailed list card (Board 01 Right).
- Readers who want to admire a volume's artwork—or flip through a cached volume like an artbook—can tap `🖼 插图 12P` to open the **Artbook & Comic Spread Viewer** directly!

### 3. Four Light Novel / Comic Reading Postures (`SingleChoiceSegmentedButtonRow`)
Inside the Reader's M3 `ModalBottomSheet` (Board 04 Right), readers can switch live between 4 postures tailored to how light novels and manga/illustrations are read:
1. **`仿真翻页` (3D Page Curl / Bunkobon Page Turn)**: Touch-tracked 3D page peel with spine shadow.
2. **`平滑覆盖` (M3 Expressive Spring Slide)**: Physics-driven horizontal page cards with velocity-aware fling.
3. **`条漫卷轴` (Continuous Webtoon / Vertical Strip Stream)**: Seamless vertical scrolling across chapters and full-width illustrations (ideal for illustration-heavy chapters and one-handed thumb scrolling).
4. **`文库竖排` (Japanese Bunkobon Right-to-Left Vertical CJK)**: Traditional vertical right-to-left columns (`「……」` vertical quotation marks, CJK *Kinsoku Shori* punctuation hanging, right-to-left page turn) matching physical Dengeki / Kadokawa bunkobon editions.

### 4. Live Translucent Typography Slider Preview + Scrubber Undo Chip
- **Android 16 Vertical-Handle `Slider`s**: Pressing and dragging the `字号大小` or `行距与段距` vertical-handle slider fades the `ModalBottomSheet` surface to **15% opacity (`85%` transparent)** using `MotionScheme.expressive().fastEffectsSpec()`, so the reader sees the actual light novel paragraphs and inline `<!--image-->` cards reflow underneath their finger in real time.
- **Scrubber `还原至第 X 页` AssistChip**: Dragging the chapter page slider pops an M3 `AssistChip` (`↩ 还原至第 4 页`) so accidental scrubs or quick peeks ahead in a volume can be undone in 1 tap.
